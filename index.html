Tue 27 OctBuild the chosen design, check the matches make sense, draft the story.
Wed 28 OctTest it as graduates, finish the README, plan the presentation.
Thu 29 OctFix the last bugs, rehearse, submit and present.

Each day starts with a 9:00 stand-up and ends with a 4:30 check-in. The detail for each day is on the project board.
