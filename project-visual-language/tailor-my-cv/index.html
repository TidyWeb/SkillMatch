Start setting up ↓
01

Your privacy

Know what the SkillMatch App uses and which files stay in your SkillMatch Folder.

SkillMatch App

No account is needed.

Your job-search profile stays in this browser tab. The SkillMatch App sends it with each search, then the server forgets it. It is not stored on the server.

The job brief contains public advert details and may include match hints based on your profile. The SkillMatch App does not read your CV or biography from your SkillMatch Folder.

Your SkillMatch Folder

Your files stay in your folder.

Your CV and biography live in the SkillMatch Folder on your computer. The SkillMatch App cannot open that folder.

The Claude desktop app reads those files when you ask it to. This page does not save your job brief; it only copies it or opens a new chat when you choose.

Claude privacy: Claude reads your files when you ask it to. Free, Pro and Max users can choose in privacy settings whether chats help improve Claude. Check Claude’s current privacy guidance before sharing sensitive details.

02

Set up once

Lay out the cloth: one CV and a biography of your working life. Put the tools in place: Claude desktop, Desktop Commander and your SkillMatch folder. Set your tailor up well once, then add to your biography whenever new stories come to mind.

  1. AUse your AI as a helper

    Talk to Claude like a colleague who understands this stuff. Tell it your problem in normal language and be specific.

    For example: Desktop Commander can't see my local files. Why not? Give me a step-by-step solution. Then wait for Claude’s reply and do one step at a time.

    Copyable prompt

    I’m setting up the SkillMatch CV Tailor.
My problem is: [describe it in normal language].
What happens is: [describe what you see].
Please give me a step-by-step solution, one step at a time. Wait for me to say I’ve done each step before giving the next.

  2. BInstall the Claude desktop app

    Install the app, not the website. The website opens in a browser with tabs and an address bar. The Claude desktop app opens in its own window and has Settings.

    Download it from claude.ai/download. It needs Windows 10 or later, macOS 13 or later, or Ubuntu 22.04 LTS or later / Debian 12 or later on x64 or arm64. Sign in; a free account is fine.

    On Windows, closing the window hides the app in the system tray by the clock. Right-click its taskbar icon and choose Pin to taskbar. If you lose it, press Start and type Anthropic.

    Ubuntu or Debian: open a terminal and run Anthropic’s apt commands below. Then open the Claude desktop app and sign in. See Anthropic’s install instructions.

    sudo curl -fsSLo /usr/share/keyrings/claude-desktop-archive-keyring.asc https://downloads.claude.ai/claude-desktop/key.asc
echo "deb [signed-by=/usr/share/keyrings/claude-desktop-archive-keyring.asc] https://downloads.claude.ai/claude-desktop/apt/stable stable main" | sudo tee /etc/apt/sources.list.d/claude-desktop.list
sudo apt update && sudo apt install claude-desktop

    If you use another Linux system, ask Claude in your browser how to install the Claude desktop app on it.

  3. CMake your SkillMatch folder and add one CV

    On Windows, make C:\SkillMatch. On Mac or Linux, make a folder called SkillMatch in your home folder.

    Do not put it on the Desktop. On many Windows computers, the Desktop syncs with OneDrive and makes the folder path harder to find.

    Put one CV in the folder, as a Word or PDF file. To update it later, save over that same file. Keep one CV in the folder.

  4. DAdd the Desktop Commander extension

    Desktop Commander lets the Claude desktop app open and save files in your SkillMatch folder, including Word and PDF.

    In the Claude desktop app, open Settings > Extensions. In newer versions, use Settings > Desktop app > Manage beside Extensions. Find Desktop Commander and install it.

    Windows: check both Desktop Commander entries. In our test, there were two entries with different icons: one looked like a chevron and slash, and one had a “DC” lettermark. One showed as installed, but Desktop Commander only worked after we installed the other entry. If it does not work, try the other entry. If it appears under Yours, that only links it to your account; make sure you install it on this computer too.

    Also open Settings > Capabilities and switch on Code execution and file creation.

    Still stuck? Use card A: tell Claude what's happening.

  5. ECheck it really worked

    In the Claude desktop app, check that Desktop Commander is installed in Settings > Extensions and switched on. Quit Claude fully, then reopen it.

    Start a new chat and ask:

    List the files in my SkillMatch folder.

    If Claude says it has no tool for your files, Desktop Commander is not installed yet. Go back and install it again.

    Limit its file access to your SkillMatch folder. Start a new chat and ask:

    Windows
    Set Desktop Commander's allowed folders to C:\SkillMatch only.
    Mac or Linux
    Set Desktop Commander's allowed folders to the SkillMatch folder in my home folder only.

    On Pro and Max plans, chats may run in the cloud and may not use local extensions. Free accounts work with local extensions.

  6. F

    Start your biography

    This is the most important step.

    Your biography is the cloth your tailor cuts from. Everything else depends on it.

    Add the SkillMatch Skill:

    • Download the SkillMatch Skill package and save it in your SkillMatch folder. Don't open or unzip it.
    • In the Claude desktop app, go to Customize > Skills. Click +, then Create skill, then Upload a skill, and choose the zip from your SkillMatch folder. The SkillMatch Skill appears in your skills list: make sure its toggle is on. (No Skills section? Switch on Code execution and file creation in Settings > Capabilities, as in card D.)

    Before you start: your training-data setting

    Your biography holds personal stories. In the Claude desktop app, open Settings > Privacy and find Help improve Claude. Switch it off if you don't want your chats used to train Claude's future models; leave it on if you're happy to help. Free, Pro and Max users can all choose. See Anthropic's guide to this setting.

    Start a new chat and say:

    run SkillMatch

    Claude asks about real stories from your working life, one question at a time. About 20–30 minutes covers two or three jobs. Your biography is saved in your SkillMatch folder.

    Come back any time:

    • carry on with my biography
    • add to my biography: … when you remember something

    The bigger it grows, the more cloth it has, and the better the tailor works.

04

How to use it for a job

Your setup is ready. Repeat these four actions for each job you want to explore.

Your job brief

Open a job in the SkillMatch App and choose Tailor my CV. Or paste a job advert into the Claude desktop app with this first line:

Tailor my CV for this job using my SkillMatch folder.








Open in Claude desktop

Your job details, ready to paste into the Claude desktop app.






If the clipboard is blocked, select the text in the box and press Ctrl+C (Mac: Cmd+C).








01 · Find
Open the SkillMatch app and choose a job.


02 · Copy
Press Tailor my CV to bring the job brief here.


03 · Paste
Copy the brief and paste it into Claude desktop.


04 · Save
Your tailored CV is saved in your SkillMatch folder.






Then, in Claude


Claude uses your CV and biography to do the writing; it never invents experience. The SkillMatch Skill checks whether the job looks worth applying for, shows what matches and what is missing, then asks whether you want suggestions or a full rewrite.


Files go in Tailored CVs/<Employer>_<Job>/ in your SkillMatch folder:


    

  • Suggestions: suggestions.md.
    • 

  • Full CV: CV.docx, CV.pdf and what_changed.md.
    • 



Gaps are listed honestly. They are never filled in on your CV.

Stuck? Ask Claude


Every computer is different. If something here does not match what you see, ask Claude. Using AI to solve problems is the skill this course is about.






If the Claude desktop app will not install or open, use Claude in your web browser at claude.ai.




Copy this help prompt





I'm setting up [what you're trying to do].
I clicked [what you clicked].
What happened: [what you saw, including any error message word for word].
My computer: [Windows 11 / Mac / Linux, and version if you know it].
Ask me questions one at a time if anything isn't clear. Give me one step at a time and wait for me to say it's done.


You can paste a screenshot too.


Never paste passwords, bank details or your full CV into a help chat.
