# Biographer lenses (draft)

A lens is an angle the Biographer uses to look at a job. For every job, it picks a concrete scenario through each lens, then asks for a STAR story (Situation, Task, Action, Result, and what you learned).

Skill ids in brackets come from `skillmatch-app/data/taxonomy/competencies.yaml`, so the biography uses the same words as the job matching.

Lenses 8 and 9 are new suggestions, added so every competency has a home. Keep or cut.

## 1. Pressure
Rushes, deadlines, emergencies, too much at once.
- Ask: "Tell me about your busiest or most stressful shift. What did you do first?"
- Skills: problem_solving_pressure, organisation_prioritising

## 2. People
Customers, the public, patients, colleagues, explaining things, calming people down, persuading.
- Ask: "Tell me about a difficult customer or colleague. How did you handle them?"
- Also ask: "Tell me about a time someone was upset, worried or challenging. How did you respond, and what happened next?"
- Skills: emotional_support_deescalation (Biographer only), customer_service, stakeholder_management, clear_communication, communicating_non_technical, negotiation, customer_insight, teamwork, technical_writing

## 3. Rules and risk
Safety, law, audits, inspections, procedures, confidential information.
- Ask: "What rules could you never break in that job? Tell me about a time it mattered."
- Skills: regulatory_compliance, following_procedures, confidential_data, attention_to_detail

## 4. Numbers and records
Counting, measuring, money, stock, logs, targets, anything written down or tracked.
- Ask: "What did you have to count, measure or record? Did anyone use those figures?"
- Skills: numerical_confidence, data_entry_records, kpi_monitoring, budget_cost_control, stock_inventory_supply, commercial_awareness, analytical_thinking

## 5. Planning
Sequencing work, scheduling, rotas, routes, juggling several jobs or sites.
- Ask: "How did you plan a normal day or week? What happened when the plan changed?"
- Skills: scheduling_rota_planning, organisation_prioritising, project_management, multi_site_coordination

## 6. Fixing and improving
The richest lens. It has five angles:
1. **Something broke.** How you found the cause, not just the quick patch.
2. **A workaround.** Making do when the right tool, part or person wasn't there.
3. **A better way.** Changing a process or workflow so it was faster, safer or less wasteful. Lean, Kaizen, 5S (sort, set in order, shine, standardise, sustain), cutting waste.
4. **A better layout.** Rearranging a workspace, store, van, kitchen or shelf so work flowed better.
5. **Getting others to change.** Persuading colleagues or managers to adopt the new way.

Always follow up with: "How did you know it worked? Did anything get measured before and after?" A before-and-after measure is data analysis in miniature, so it's the strongest bridge into the new career.
- Ask: "Tell me about something you changed because it annoyed you or wasted time."
- Skills: process_improvement, problem_solving_pressure, analytical_thinking, change_management

## 7. Teaching and leading
Showing new starters the ropes, running a team or shift, reporting upwards.
- Ask: "Did you ever train someone or take charge? What did you do?"
- Skills: training_others, team_leadership, reporting_to_leadership

## 8. Learning and adapting (new)
Picking up a new system, rule change, tool or job fast. Career changers need this most.
- Ask: "Tell me about a time you had to learn something new quickly."
- Skills: adaptability_learning, research_methods

## 9. Trust and working alone (new)
Being left to get on with it: lone working, keys, cash, sensitive information.
- Ask: "What were you trusted with that others weren't?"
- Skills: independent_working, confidential_data

## 10. Spotting change (new)
Noticing something drift from normal (a patient, a machine, a delivery, product quality, a pattern) and deciding whether to check, record or raise it. This is the analyst's instinct, so push it hard.
- Ask: "What changed from normal in your work, how did you notice, and what did you do next?"
- Skills: situational_monitoring (Biographer only), attention_to_detail, analytical_thinking

## Skills used only by the Biographer
These ids are not in the app's `competencies.yaml` yet. Add them there later, with advert phrases.
- `situational_monitoring`: noticing a meaningful change in a person, equipment or process, checking what it means, and recording or raising it.
- `emotional_support_deescalation`: listening to someone distressed or confrontational, staying calm, and reducing tension or reassuring them.

## Rules for every lens
- Make the question concrete for that job. Never ask "Tell me about your communication skills."
- Suggest, then confirm. A skill goes in only with the user's own example.
- If a lens doesn't fit a job, skip it. Not every job has every lens.
