Research snapshot: 16 October 2026.

This catalogue contains 96 source records and 15 structured vacancy examples. These are places to investigate or monitor, not 96 independent feeds or proof of that many suitable jobs. Some are official employer watchlists, historical evidence or unresolved leads. No current vacancy total is claimed.

The source of truth for structured records is source_register.json. See Search Method and Coverage for scope, status definitions, gaps and the repeatable research process. The earlier inventory is retained as a prior research snapshot.

Findings that change the source search

Search beyond junior data analyst: MI, reporting, performance, data quality, CRM/database work, research, monitoring/evaluation, operational insight and selected junior engineering can use the graduates' skills. Assess tasks and requirements rather than title alone. An experienced example can prove a relevant source without proving candidate fit.

Local and remote searches are complementary. Employers anywhere may be relevant when a specific role permits UK-based working. Unknown country eligibility remains unknown. Hybrid is a separate case with an actual office and attendance requirements.

Reed and CV-Library deserve official access evaluation; CharityJob, IT Jobs Watch, CWJobs and graduate boards deserve supply evaluation regardless of API availability. Robert Walters and Amtis have stronger inspected regional evidence than the initial trawl found. WWR offers an explicitly documented public RSS route, but suitable UK-eligible analyst supply is untested. API ease is not a measure of vacancy relevance.

Conditions for future application use

The JSON is a research register, not a working feed. Every record has collection disabled. Before a source becomes a live input, confirm the authorised route, actual relevant supply, allowed storage/display/analysis, attribution, limits, vacancy expiry, and how source IDs map to duplicate adverts. Keep manual/source-link discovery for useful sources without an agreed feed. Do not lose them from the library.

Salary is optional. Keep missing salaries unknown, with advertised annual/hourly/day amounts distinct. Remote, experience and qualifications also need unknown states. Historical or unclear adverts must not silently become live recommendations.
