## My Contribution to SkillMatch

During the SkillMatch hackathon (26–30 October 2026), I contributed to the original concept and the development of the wider product.

My initial idea was to help people find suitable jobs in their preferred locations while showing them how their existing skills and experience could help them achieve their career goals.

## My main areas of involvement included:

Project coordination: Helping organise team communication, understand individual responsibilities, monitor progress and maintain momentum throughout the hackathon.

Product concept: Contributing to the original idea of matching jobseekers with opportunities based on transferable skills, rather than relying exclusively on previous job titles.

Data-driven development: Working with the data analytics workstream to explore how labour-market insights could inform the application and its matching approach.

Matching algorithm: Investigating the existing matching logic and exploring potential improvements, including implementing a small change on a separate development branch.

AI-assisted development: Using tools such as ChatGPT Projects and DeepSeek to understand the codebase, investigate technical approaches and support implementation.
