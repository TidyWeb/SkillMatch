Step 1 of 2

Try the prototype

Run the working SkillMatch prototype in your browser. Nothing to install. About 5 minutes the first time.

  1. Open it Click the button, then Create codespace. It runs on GitHub's computers, not yours.

    Open SkillMatch in Codespaces

  2. Wait for it to start The first time takes a few minutes while it installs and builds its adverts. A new tab opens with SkillMatch. If it doesn't, open the Ports tab at the bottom and click the globe next to SkillMatch.
  3. Try it Click one of the Try a demo profile buttons, or fill in your own background, then See my matches. Open a job to see how each requirement is met, or what's missing.

These adverts are made up

The footer says Fixtures · synthetic adverts: 119 adverts from fictional employers, and their links go to dummy pages. That's because we don't yet have Reed's permission to share real adverts. Want to see real West Midlands jobs? B2 shows you how, with your own free key.

Done when: you've seen a list of matches and opened one job. To come back later, go to github.com/codespaces. Your codespace stops by itself after 30 minutes idle.
