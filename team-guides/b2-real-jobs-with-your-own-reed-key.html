See real jobs with your own Reed key

Swap the made-up adverts for live West Midlands jobs from Reed, using your own free key. About 45 minutes, most of it waiting.

  1. Get a free Reed key Register at reed.co.uk/developers. Your key arrives by email. It's private: never paste it into a file, a chat or Discord.
  2. Save it in GitHub Your picture → Settings → Codespaces → New secret. Name: REED_API_KEY. Value: your key. Under Repository access, choose TidyWeb/SkillMatch. Click Add secret.
  3. Restart your codespace Secrets only load when a codespace starts. At github.com/codespaces, click … next to yours → Stop codespace, then open it again. (No codespace yet? Just do B1 now.)
  4. Fetch the real jobs In the terminal at the bottom, run:
    cd prototype/app && uv run python -m skillmatch.etl --mode live
    It takes 25–40 minutes. Leave the tab open. It's finished when it prints a quality report line.
  5. Restart once more Stop and reopen the codespace as in step 3, so the app loads the new jobs.

Real adverts are for trying the app only. They stay inside your own codespace. Don't copy them into the repository, Discord or the presentation until Reed has said yes.

Done when: the footer says Live snapshot with today's date, and job links open on reed.co.uk.
