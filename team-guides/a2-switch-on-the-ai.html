Step 2 of 4

Pick your AI assistant

Use whichever AI assistant you already like to do the typing for you. Any of them will do, as long as it can work on your SkillMatch folder. About 5 minutes.

  1. Choose your assistant Use one you already know, such as Claude, Codex or Gemini, or one built into your editor. It needs to do two things: see the files in your SkillMatch folder, and run commands on your computer, asking you first. A chat window where you only copy and paste text can't do the second.
  2. Point it at your SkillMatch folder Open the SkillMatch folder you cloned in A1, and start your assistant from there (or tell it to work in that folder).
  3. Test it Send this, and nothing more:
    Which branch is this repository on, and is anything uncommitted? Don't change anything.
    A good answer names a branch (probably main) and says whether anything is uncommitted. It should not edit a file or ask to run anything risky.

No assistant that can do this? You can still do every step by hand. The help page shows how to add your folder without one.

Done when: your assistant has correctly told you which branch SkillMatch is on.
