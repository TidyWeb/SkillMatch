Step 1 of 3

Try the prototype

Run the working SkillMatch prototype in your browser. Nothing to install. About 5 minutes the first time.

  1. Open it Click the button, then Create codespace. It runs on GitHub's computers, not yours.

    Open SkillMatch in Codespaces

  2. Trust it, then wait It opens looking like VS Code. That's normal, and you don't need to touch any code or the README that opens. If it asks whether you trust the folder or its authors, choose Yes, I trust. The terminal at the bottom shows the set-up running (installing, preparing packages, building the adverts). That takes a few minutes the first time.
  3. Open the app When the terminal says Running on http://127.0.0.1:5000, hold Ctrl (Windows or Linux) or Cmd (Mac) and click that address. SkillMatch opens in a new tab. Ignore the other addresses it lists. Copying them into your browser won't work: they only exist inside GitHub's computer, and the click gives you a private link to the app instead.
  4. Try it Click one of the Try a demo profile buttons, or fill in your own background, then See my matches. Open a job to see how each requirement is met, or what's missing.

These adverts are made up

The footer says Fixtures · synthetic adverts: 119 adverts from fictional employers, and their links go to dummy pages. That's because we don't yet have Reed's permission to share real adverts. Want to see real West Midlands jobs? C2 shows you how, with your own free key.

Done when: you've seen a list of matches and opened one job. To come back later, go to github.com/codespaces. Your codespace stops by itself after 30 minutes idle.
