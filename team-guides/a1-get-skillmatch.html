Step 1 of 4

Get SkillMatch onto your machine

Put a copy of the team repository on the computer you've used for the course, in its own folder. About 5 minutes.

First: accept Phil's invitation to TidyWeb/SkillMatch (it's in your email, or at github.com/notifications). Without it you can look, but you can't upload.

  1. Choose where it lives Use the folder where you keep your course projects. If you don't have one, make Documents/Projects.
  2. Clone it in VS Code Source Control (Ctrl+Shift+G, Mac Cmd+Shift+G) → Clone Repository → paste this → pick your projects folder:
    https://github.com/TidyWeb/SkillMatch.git
  3. Open it Click Open when VS Code asks. If it asks whether you trust the authors, say yes.

Done when: the Explorer shows Phil's Folder, Research and README.md, and the bottom-left corner says main.
