Step 3 of 5

Save and upload

Save your work as a checkpoint (a commit), then upload your branch to GitHub. Do it at least once a day.

  1. Make your change and save the files
  2. Choose what goes in In Source Control, look at Changes. Click + beside only the files you meant to change.
  3. Commit with a clear message Say what you did, in plain words, then click Commit:
    Add interface feedback for prototype B
  4. Upload Click Publish Branch the first time, then Sync Changes after that.

Prefer the AI to do it? In Copilot (Agent), paste:

Show me what has changed. Stage only the files I confirm, commit them with a short plain-English message I approve, and push this branch. Never push to main or force-push.

Done when: Source Control shows no outstanding changes, and your branch appears on GitHub.
