# Changelog

All notable changes. Dates are when the change was made.

## v0.2 (unreleased): 21 October 2026

- Insights page: headline figures with n; competencies and skills most asked for in entry-plausible adverts; salary bands; senior roles hidden by word; working arrangements; a map coloured by fit and charts based on your profile. Every chart has a table view.
- Sources page (three tiers, provider findings, CV-Library registration link) and About page (how matching works, limitations, privacy).
- Analysis notebook with two hypothesis tests (chi-square or Fisher's exact), competency demand and limitations, generated by `scripts/build_notebook.py`.
- Container: `python:3.12-slim`, gunicorn, web-only dependencies (~150 MB, ready in about 1 s).
- GitHub Actions: nightly `refresh-and-deploy.yml` (live ETL, tests, build, Cloud Run via Workload Identity Federation) and `ci.yml`.
- Self-check script; a Strong fit now needs at least two essentials we can check; "Experience required" headings recognised; tidier answers and duplicate links.
- Ranking now prioritises match tier (Strong, Good, Stretch, Not yet), then distance, then title.
- Added `tests/test_ranking.py` to cover tier-first ordering, distance and title tie-breakers, and missing distances.
- Docs: README, architecture, deploy, taxonomy review guide, final report.

## v0.1: 21 October 2026

- Profile page (single form, works at 380px): skills with three levels, up to four previous roles with suggested competencies and evidence lines, six demo personas. Kept in sessionStorage only.
- Results grouped Strong fit / Good fit / Stretch / Not yet, nearest first, with filters, hidden counts you can reveal, and "Also search these sources near you" links.
- Job page: requirement-by-requirement table with the advert's sentence, our reading and how your profile answers it.
- ETL for fixtures and live mode: Reed, Greenhouse, Lever, Workable, We Work Remotely, Remote OK, curated adverts and a CV-Library stub. Geocoding, filters, parsing, de-duplication, SQLite and a quality report.
- Taxonomy (51 skills, 40 competencies, 40 role archetypes, phrasing templates) with validation.
- Calibration on the live Reed snapshot of 21 October 2026 (before and after numbers in DECISIONS.md).
