# Your part: data analysis, what the search found (3 min)

A guide for Monday 2 November, not a script. Use your own words, and add detail or examples as you like.

Before you: SK talks about Tailor my CV.
After you: Oana finishes the data analysis (advertisers, pay, the test, what next).

- The question: do the skills the course teaches match the skills employers ask for?
- Your radar chart was the first answer: employer demand against course coverage.
- One captured week, 30 October, West Midlands:
  - 24,656 search appearances (an advert can appear more than once).
  - 4,654 distinct adverts collected.
  - 1,988 data-role adverts.
  - 1,512 local ones, then 85 after the seniority filter.
  - 67 matched to a graduate.
- Adverts come and go fast: 148 new, 158 gone by the next collection. Median time seen: 3.25 days.
- Skills employers ask for: in adverts with full descriptions, SQL is essential in 60 and Excel in 42. Neither is marked as covered in the course.
- Tools: notebook in Google Colab, then summary data exported for the public analysis page.
- Hand over: "Oana will finish the analysis."

## Be ready for

- Is this the whole job market? No. One captured sample of one search.
- Why 24,656 but only 4,654? Search appearances count repeats. The 4,654 are distinct adverts.
- How is "covered by the course" decided? It is the project's course-coverage model. SQL and Excel are marked as gaps in it.
- Why so few matches? Most collected adverts are not entry level, not local, or not data roles.
